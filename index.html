01

The driver sees too late

By the time danger is noticed by eye, there is no time left to brake — human reaction is measured in seconds.

02

Blind spots on the road

Fog, dust, night and sharp turns cut visibility drastically — cameras become unreliable in exactly these conditions.

03

Cities run without data

There is no continuous, real-time data on congestion and pedestrian safety to manage the city with.

The Solution

A radar that knows the danger before it is visible.

SafeVision is a 24 GHz mmWave radar module installed in the car. It continuously measures the road ahead, detects dangerous situations early, and warns the driver.

⏱

Early warning

Danger 15 seconds ahead

The radar detects obstacles and dangerous approaches up to 50 m away, so the driver has time to brake.

Smart city

What a camera cannot do

Hazard-detection accuracy measured in prototype testing.

How it works

Four steps: from wave to warning.

  1. 1

    Radar scans

    The 24 GHz antenna continuously scans the field ahead and measures the returning signal.

  2. 2

    Controller analyses

    The microcontroller computes distance, speed and closing rate to assess the level of danger.

  3. 3

    Driver is warned

    When danger is detected, an audible and visual alert is issued — on average 15 seconds ahead.

  4. 4

    Data reaches the centre

    Anonymous data is sent to the city control panel over the GSM module.

Roadmap

A 12-month plan for the incubation period.

  1. Months 1–3

    Team & design

    Complete the team; prepare the CAD model and technical drawings of the radar device; purchase components.

  2. Months 4–6

    10 prototypes

    Assemble 10 devices, 3D-print the housings, write the software and install on 10 cars.

  3. Months 7–9

    Field tests

    Collect data in real road conditions, tune the detection algorithm, reduce the false-alarm rate.

  4. Months 10–12

    Pilot & series

    Pilot project with a city authority, the certification process, and preparation for a 1,000-unit series.
